Ye page kya batata hai?

1 · Nifty ne kya kiya

2 Nov ko Nifty 23,440 par band hua, pichhle din se 1.68% upar.

Ye pichhle 20 din ke sabse unche level (23,146) se bhi upar hai. Aise mein market mein tezi ka mood maana jaata hai, par ye pakka nahi ki aage bhi chadhega.

2 · Kal kis level par nazar rakhein

23,146 ek "line" hai.

Nifty iske upar tika rahe to tezi bani hui hai. Wapas iske neeche aa jaaye to aaj ki tezi ka signal kamzor pad gaya. Ye sirf dekhne ka level hai, kharidne-bechne ki salah nahi.

3 · Kal ka time-table

9:15 khulega, 3:30 band.

Pehle 15 minute (9:15–9:30) sirf dekho. Naya trade 3:00 ke baad mat lo. Jo trade khula ho, 3:15 tak band kar do.

Neeche ka har hissa inhi teen baaton ko detail mein dikhata hai. Koi shabd samajh na aaye to sabse neeche shabdkosh dekho.

Nifty = India ki 50 sabse badi companies ka ek saath score. Chart mein narangi line Nifty ka roz ka closing level hai. Dotted line pichhle 20 din ka sabse uncha level hai: narangi line uske upar gayi matlab naya high.

Close 20-din high 20-din average 2 Nov (sabse naya din)

2 Nov: opening range breakout

Narangi dabba = pehle 15 minute mein Nifty kitna upar-neeche gaya. 9:40 par Nifty is dabbe ke upar nikal gaya (hara gola). Isi ko "breakout" kehte hain.

Index ko seedha kharida nahi ja sakta. Nifty futures ya options mein trade hota hai, aur ye tools F&O order nahi bhejte. Inhe chart aur levels padhne ke liye use karo. Options mein poora premium doob sakta hai.

Mushkil shabd, aasan matlab

Nifty 50
India ki 50 badi companies ka milaa-jula score. Ye upar gaya matlab zyadatar badi companies ke share upar gaye.
Close / band hona
3:30 baje market band hone par jo daam tha. Chart har din ka yahi daam jodta hai.
20-din high
Pichhle 20 trading dinon mein sabse uncha closing daam. Isse upar band hona = naya high.
20-din average
Pichhle 20 din ke closing daam ka ausat. Daam iske upar = haal mein tezi, neeche = mandi.
RSI
0 se 100 ka meter jo batata hai ki share haal mein kitni tezi se chadha ya gira. 70 se upar = bahut chadh chuka, 30 se neeche = bahut gir chuka.
Setup
Chart par ek jaana-pehchana pattern. Ye sirf ishara hai, guarantee nahi.
Opening range
Market khulne ke pehle 15 minute (9:15–9:30) ka sabse uncha aur sabse neecha daam.
Breakout
Jab daam kisi range ya purane high ke upar nikal kar band ho.
Stop-loss
Wo daam jahan aap pehle se tay karte ho ki galat nikle to bech denge, taaki nuksaan chhota rahe.
Target
Wo daam jahan aap munafa le kar nikal jaoge.
Day trade
Usi din khareedna aur usi din bechna. Raat bhar position nahi rakhte.
Paper trading
Nakli paise se practice. Asli paisa lagane se pehle zaroor karo.

Financial advice nahi hai. Ye software free public data padhta hai aur hisaab lagata hai. Day trading mein zyadatar log paisa gawaate hain. Pehle paper trading karo.
